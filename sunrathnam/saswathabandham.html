About Saswatha Bandham

Saswatha Bandham is a platform published by SUN RATHNA. It is intended to help prospective brides, grooms and their families discover potential matches through member profiles, profile review, compatibility information and member interaction.

Important: Saswatha Bandham facilitates profile discovery and compatibility information. It does not guarantee a marriage, the accuracy of every member-submitted statement or the suitability of a prospective match. Members and families should independently verify identity, background, employment and family information before making decisions.
